# Council

The Council is a governed organization of domain professionals used when a decision benefits from multiple expert viewpoints.

Council members are not generic personas. Each member represents a professional discipline, responsibility, or philosophy relevant to the decision.

## Operating rules

- assignments have a concrete question
- findings must identify evidence
- uncertainty and assumptions are explicit
- members may disagree
- adversarial analysis can be enabled for any assignment
- additional specialists may be added when a gap is discovered
- the Council informs decisions; it does not bypass governance

## Output

A Council result should distinguish:

- findings
- evidence
- assumptions
- risks
- disagreements
- recommended next investigations

The goal is structured professional scrutiny rather than consensus theater.
