# Elenchus

Elenchus is GSA's unknown-discovery function.

Its job is not to generate another answer. Its job is to identify what the system does not yet know well enough to proceed safely or confidently.

## It looks for

- hidden assumptions
- missing requirements
- unsupported claims
- stale evidence
- contradictory facts
- unresolved dependencies
- unverified external conditions
- decisions made without sufficient context

## Output

Elenchus turns uncertainty into explicit work items with status, owner or investigation path, and evidence requirements.

Unknowns remain visible until resolved, waived, or made irrelevant by a changed plan.
