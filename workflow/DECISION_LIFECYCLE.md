# Decision Lifecycle

Decisions in GSA are explicit objects with context and consequences.

## Lifecycle

1. **Question** — what decision is being made?
2. **Context** — which state, requirements, and constraints apply?
3. **Unknowns** — what is unresolved?
4. **Investigation** — what evidence or specialist review is needed?
5. **Proposal** — what action or conclusion is recommended?
6. **Governance** — is the proposed action authorized?
7. **Execution** — perform the bounded operation.
8. **Verification** — test the actual result.
9. **Outcome** — record what happened.
10. **History** — preserve the decision, evidence, and consequence.

A decision can later be superseded without erasing the rationale that produced it.
