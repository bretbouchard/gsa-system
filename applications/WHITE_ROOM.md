# White Room

[White Room System](https://github.com/bretbouchard/white-room-system) applies GSA principles to a real-time creative/music environment.

The domain differs from Volta: White Room is optimized for fluid creative interaction rather than manufacturing release. Even so, the same architectural rules matter—authoritative project state outside the model, explicit tools, durable preferences and plans, bounded capabilities, evidence-backed changes, and model interchangeability.

This makes White Room a useful demonstration that GSA is not tied to one engineering domain.
