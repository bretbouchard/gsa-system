# White Room

[White Room System](https://github.com/bretbouchard/white-room-system) applies GSA principles to a real-time creative/music environment.

The domain differs from Volta: White Room is optimized for fluid creative interaction rather than manufacturing release. Even so, the same architectural rules matter—authoritative project state outside the model, explicit tools, durable preferences and plans, bounded capabilities, evidence-backed changes, model interchangeability, and outcome validation.

## Reality Feedback

White Room contributes domain-specific observations into GSA's shared Reality Feedback Loop rather than implementing its own parallel feedback system.

Potential evidence includes:

- crashes and regressions;
- DSP failures, glitches, underruns, and performance measurements;
- repeated corrective user actions;
- workflow friction and abandoned paths;
- musical preferences and recurring creative choices;
- mixer, instrument, and effect interaction patterns;
- render and playback discrepancies;
- publishing or export failures;
- explicit user feedback.

These signals are evidence, not automatic product requirements. They may create investigations that Elenchus, Council, Historian, GSD, and Obdurate process through normal governance.

A White Room Change can therefore be implementation-verified while its product or creative outcome remains unobserved, inconclusive, or failed.

This makes White Room a useful demonstration that GSA is not tied to one engineering domain and that a self-correcting product does not need to collapse user behavior directly into autonomous code changes.
